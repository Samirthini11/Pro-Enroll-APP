# 08 · Launch & Growth Roadmap

This roadmap focuses on the pilot region — **Pondicherry + Karaikal** —
with adjacent Tamil Nadu towns (Cuddalore, Villupuram, Tindivanam,
Panruti, Neyveli) added as the platform stabilises.

The roadmap is written in **phases**, not weeks or months — pace
depends on hiring, capital, and how fast verified supply can be built.

---

## Phase 0 · Foundations

**Outcome:** Codebase, brand, and infra are ready; a small private beta
is running.

- Register company, GST, Razorpay, MSG91, KYC provider accounts.
- Lock the two app brands (Pro‑Enroll, Pro‑User), logos, Play/App store
  listings, Tamil + English store copy.
- Build MVP scope (see `01-product-overview.md` § 3 and
  `02-feature-suggestions.md` § A).
- Stand up the MVP server (see `04-server-cost-and-specifications.md`).
- Recruit 20 – 30 pros from Pondicherry by physically walking through
  Mission Street, Lawspet, Reddiarpalayam, and partnering with two
  electronics shops.
- Recruit 50 – 100 friendly customers (founders’ network, residential
  WhatsApp groups, college students).
- Run **private beta** invitations only.

**Success bar:** 50 verified bookings completed end‑to‑end, average
rating ≥ 4.3, zero unresolved payment disputes.

---

## Phase 1 · Pondicherry public launch

**Outcome:** Anyone in Pondicherry city can open the app and book a
verified pro.

### Supply (Pro side)

- Onboarding camps at **3 hubs**: Mission Street, Villianur road,
  Lawspet. Field agents help pros install the app, scan Aadhaar, take
  selfie, sign T&C in Tamil.
- Partner with the **Pondicherry AC Dealers Association** and **RO
  service shops** — owners list their team via a bulk‑enrol screen.
- Run a Tamil short video on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts:
  “No more middleman. Get jobs directly. Daily payout to UPI.”
- **Referral bonus:** ₹100 to the referring pro + ₹100 to the new pro
  once the new pro completes 5 jobs.
- Target **500 verified pros across 8 categories**: AC, plumber,
  electrician, RO, fridge, washing machine, car mechanic, bike mechanic.

### Demand (Customer side)

- Tamil + English Google Ads on hyperlocal search terms (“AC service
  Pondicherry”, “plumber near me Pondy”).
- Tie up with **5 residential associations** (Auroville, Reddiarpalayam,
  Lawspet) — QR code at the gate gets residents ₹100 off first booking.
- Sticker campaign at 30 popular tea shops & 50 auto‑rickshaws.
- One‑time radio spot on **Suriyan FM 93.5** during summer (peak AC
  demand).
- WhatsApp Business account with auto‑replies; customers can also book
  via WhatsApp by typing the category.

**Success bar (Phase 1):**
- 800 verified pros, 8 000 customer installs.
- 1 200 bookings/month, ≥ 4.4 average rating.
- Repeat‑booking rate ≥ 25 %.
- Net contribution per booking ≥ ₹ 40 (see unit economics in `04`).

## Phase 2 · Karaikal + Cuddalore expansion

**Outcome:** Two new towns live; cross‑category bookings start to
emerge (a customer who booked AC also books a plumber).

- Repeat the Pondicherry onboarding playbook in Karaikal town centre,
  Bharathiar Road and Thirunallar.
- Cuddalore launch focuses on **2‑wheeler & car mechanics** and
  **appliance repair** — different demand profile than Pondy.
- Launch the **₹999/year Home AMC plan** (2 AC services + 2 RO filter
  changes + 1 plumbing visit). Sold via in‑app banner and WhatsApp
  campaign — drives high retention.
- Launch **Featured Listing** for pros (₹20 – ₹50/day boost). First
  monetisation lever beyond commission.
- Add **Tamil voice search** and **Tamil voice‑to‑book** in the user app.

**Success bar (Phase 2):**
- 2 500 verified pros across 3 cities.
- 4 000 bookings/month.
- 800 active AMC subscribers (~₹ 8 L revenue locked).
- Pro churn (active → inactive) < 10 % / month.

## Phase 3 · Coastal Tamil Nadu corridor

**Outcome:** Coverage from Pondicherry → Cuddalore → Chidambaram →
Karaikal → Nagapattinam → Thanjavur. Brand becomes recognised as the
“verified local services” app in southern coastal TN.

- Add **construction & carpentry** categories.
- Roll out **Pro Academy** (Tamil video courses + certificate). Drives
  ₹99/month subscription uptake.
- Launch **spare‑parts marketplace** for pros (AC gas, RO membranes,
  bike parts).
- Tie‑up with **2 NBFCs** for pro equipment loans.
- Hire local city managers (1 per city) for offline operations.

**Success bar:**
- 8 000 verified pros, 200 K MAU.
- 10 000 bookings/month.
- GMV ₹ 75 L / month, net revenue ₹ 9 L / month.

---

## Phase 4 · Multi‑city Tamil Nadu + Puducherry UT

**Outcome:** Live in 10+ cities. Defensible moat: verified supply, AMC
subscribers, training, spare parts.

- Add **beauty‑at‑home**, **home cleaning**, **pest control**, **home
  tutors**.
- Launch B2B: property managers, Airbnb hosts (huge in Pondy),
  hostels, schools.
- Open APIs for partners (insurance companies, white goods brands
  like LG / Samsung / Voltas for warranty service).

**Success bar:**
- 50 000 pros, 1 M MAU.
- 60 000 bookings/month.
- Series‑A‑ready financials.

## Risks & mitigations (operational)

| Risk                                          | Mitigation                                                |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cyclone / power cuts disrupt service          | Have offline call centre fallback; reschedule auto-flow.  |
| Local strongmen / mafia interference          | Build relationships with police & merchant associations; transparent pricing reduces conflict. |
| Pros poaching customers off‑platform          | Masked numbers, loyalty wallet, AMC plans, training perks.|
| Customer says “job not done”                  | Before/after photos + GPS proof; dispute SLA 4 h.         |
| Cash‑on‑service collection fraud              | Pro app shows expected amount; OTP at end of job to confirm cash received. |
| Aadhaar / data‑breach exposure                | Encrypt at rest, only last 4 digits stored, KYC docs auto‑deleted on offboarding. |
| Cannibalisation by big player entering        | Verified supply + AMC subscribers + Tamil‑first UX form the moat. |

## North‑star metrics

1. **Verified bookings per active customer per quarter** — proxy for
   product‑market fit; target ≥ 1.4 by Phase 2.
2. **Pro retention (90‑day)** — % of pros still completing jobs 90 days
   after enrollment; target ≥ 60 %.
3. **Repeat‑booking rate** — % of customers who book twice in 90 days;
   target ≥ 30 %.
4. **AMC active subscribers** — strongest lifetime‑value indicator.
5. **Customer NPS (Tamil)** — measured monthly via in‑app survey;
   target ≥ 55.
